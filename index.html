Welcome to Kekulama

Built from the garden it sits in.

Kekulama began as a family coconut plot. The cabanas came later — timber frames, cadjan thatch, and wide verandahs raised on stilts so the breeze runs underneath. Nothing here is air-freighted: the furniture is local mahogany offcuts, the water is filtered rain, and the compost goes back into the beds that feed the kitchen.

It is quiet. That is rather the point.

Meet the cabanas

Hand-built cabanas

Cadjan roofs, mosquito-netted beds, outdoor rain showers and a verandah hammock in every one.

Garden kitchen & bar

Rice and curry cooked over firewood, seafood from Galle harbour, and arrack sours on the deck at dusk.

Low-impact by habit

Solar hot water, rain harvesting, no single-use plastic, and a garden that composts its own kitchen.

Where you sleep

Our cabanas

Six in total, spaced far enough apart that you will hear birds before you hear neighbours. Rates include breakfast, tax and the airport-free conscience.

Around us

Things to do

Everything below is arranged at the desk, with drivers and guides we actually know. Distances are from our gate.

Galle Fort ramparts

Dutch walls, lighthouse, and the best hour of the day for a walk. Tuk-tuk arranged in ten minutes.

10 min · Year round

Whale watching, Mirissa

Early boat out past the shelf for blue whales and spinner dolphins. We pack breakfast for the drive.

50 min · Nov – Apr

Koggala mangrove paddle

Kayak the lagoon channels at first light, past cinnamon islands and the stilt fishermen.

20 min · Sunrise & sunset

Turtle hatchery, Habaraduwa

A small conservation hatchery on the coast road — best visited late afternoon, before the release.

15 min · Year round
